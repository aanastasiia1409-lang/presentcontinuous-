The rule: Present Continuous

We use Present Continuous for actions that are happening now. Form: am / is / are + verb-ing.

  • I am playing. She is reading. They are swimming.
  • Most verbs: add -ing. play → playing
  • Verbs ending in -e: drop the e. dance → dancing, make → making
  • Short verbs (consonant, vowel, consonant): double the last letter. run → running, sit → sitting
  • Negative: add not. He is not (isn't) sleeping.
  • Question: put am / is / are first. Are you eating?
🥪 «Грамматический Бутерброд»
Запомни это время как бутерброд: am / is / are (верхний хлеб) + глагол (начинка) + -ing (нижний хлеб).
She is eating = 🍞 is + eat + ing 🍞
